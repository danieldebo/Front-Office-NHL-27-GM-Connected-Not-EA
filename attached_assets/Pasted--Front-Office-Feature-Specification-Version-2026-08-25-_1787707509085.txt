# Front Office — Feature Specification

**Version:** 2026-08-25 (rev 3)
**Scope:** Companion platform for NHL 27 Connected Franchise leagues. The game runs the hockey; Front Office runs the league — coordination, scheduling, stats, standings, and everything EA doesn't keep.

---

## 1. Platform Core *(existing)*

- Account auth; commissioner self-signup
- Persistent footer: "Created by DeBo" with $1 donation suggestion → Venmo @theedebo
- Footer feature-request modal (optional email + message)
- Append-only fact store, derived views (standings, cap), provenance on every fact

## 2. User Profiles & Platform Identity **[NEW]**

- Profile fields: display name, email, **time zone (required — feeds scheduling)**
- **Platform identities:** Xbox Gamertag and/or PSN Online ID; one marked primary
- **Systems played:** multi-select (Xbox, PlayStation)
- **League-type gate:** joining an Xbox league requires an Xbox identity on file; PlayStation likewise; Crossplay requires at least one. Matches the league's enforced platform field and keeps discovery honest.
- Gamertags surface on team pages, rosters, and matchup cards — the in-game invite should always be one glance away
- Identity changes are logged (who, when, old → new)

## 3. League Creation & Settings Sheet

*Existing:* commissioner creates league; league type Xbox / PlayStation / Crossplay (enforced, drives discovery + matchmaking); shareable league link (public_code + /l/slug); reusable invite links (/join/token with rotation, expiry, use caps).

**[NEW] Settings Sheet** — a structured mirror of every in-game Connected Franchise configurable, so the site is the league's source of truth:

- In-game **League ID** (links the site league to its EA counterpart)
- Team count (3–32, matching EA's range) — drives seat-limit enforcement
- Roster source: default EA / updated EA / custom (custom routes through §6 Uploads)
- Schedule format, playoff format, salary cap rules, divisions & conferences, rule-set notes, gameplay slider preset (key/value or free text)
- **Versioned settings:** mid-season edits (EA allows commissioner rule tweaks) create a new version with a change log visible to all members — no silent rule changes

## 4. Membership & Provisioning *(existing)*

- Enforced team/seat limit per league, tied to Settings Sheet team count
- Commissioner provisions all members (names, emails, logos), including people without accounts yet — pre-provisioned seats are claimable on signup
- Per-member relationship tiers: friend / VIP / stranger
- Automatic weekly commissioner email built from an editable template

## 5. Discovery & Intake *(existing)*

- Open leagues page with league-type filtering
- Self-reported Ranked Division (Bronze / Silver / Gold / Diamond / Platinum / Elite / Ultimate)
- Hidden admin skill rating (10-scale, never shown to users)
- League waitlist join
- **[NEW touchpoint]** discovery can filter by the user's systems on file, showing only leagues they're actually eligible to join

## 6. Rosters, Stats & Uploads **[NEW]**

**Who uploads:** team owners *and* commissioners.

**Approval gate:**
- Member uploads land in **Pending** → commissioner approves or rejects (with note)
- League settings: **auto-approve toggles**, separate for roster uploads and stat uploads
- Commissioner uploads apply immediately (self-approved, still logged like everything else)

**Roster uploads:**
- Downloadable CSV template; validation against the NHL player registry, manual-entry fallback for custom players
- **Diff preview** before submit: adds, drops, and moves vs the current roster — the commissioner approves a diff, not a mystery file
- On approval, changes post as append-only facts (uploader, approver, timestamp, source file retained)

**Stats uploads:**
- Per-game box scores via form entry or CSV; season-level stat imports
- Same pending/approve flow; approved stats feed standings, leaders, and history

**Audit:** every upload stores uploader, timestamp, approval state, approver, and the applied diff. Nothing is overwritten.

## 7. Keepers & Player Registry *(existing)*

- Teams input keepers; commissioner sets keepers-per-team and can add/edit/remove keepers on any team
- Original keeper-designation date tracked
- Indexed registry of all NHL players seeded from the free public NHL API (api.nhle.com/stats/rest, api-web.nhle.com), manual-entry fallback
- Registry match → 1/3/5-year stat card on the team admin page

## 8. Transactions: Trades, Trade Block, Free Agency

Mirrors the in-game flow — EA routes every transaction through the commissioner, and so does Front Office:

- **Trade proposals:** up to 5 players per side (matches the in-game cap), with cap-impact preview from the derived cap view
- **Commissioner approval queue:** approve/reject with note; approved trades post as facts and notify both GMs
- **Trade Block:** list up to 5 players with stated asks (position / age / salary), visible league-wide
- **Free agency:** claim submissions, per-team priority lists, drops; commissioner processes; public claim log
- **Cap violation flag:** EA requires violations resolved before playing — dashboard warning stays lit until the team is legal

## 9. Scheduling & Notifications **[NEW — priority module]**

The gap: EA's flexible scheduling lets games be played in any order but gives zero tools for agreeing on *when*. Front Office owns this.

**Availability:**
- Recurring weekly availability windows per user, plus blackout dates
- Stored against the user's time zone, always rendered in the viewer's

**Matchups:**
- Generated from the Settings Sheet schedule format (round robin × N, division-weighted) or imported via CSV for custom schedules
- Every matchup card shows both gamertags and platform — no hunting for who to invite

**Scheduling flow:**
1. Either player proposes up to 3 time slots (smart suggestions pulled from availability overlap)
2. Opponent accepts one or counters
3. Confirmed slot locks the game; both parties notified with an .ics attached

**Reschedules & no-shows:**
- Reschedule request → other party accepts or declines; full audit trail
- No-show reporting after a grace period → commissioner resolution queue (assign forfeit = force 1-0, mirroring EA's override tools)

**Deadlines & nudges:**
- Commissioner sets play-by windows (e.g., every matchup within 7 days)
- Overdue games auto-flag on the commissioner dashboard and trigger nudge notifications to both players

**Calendar:**
- Per-user **ICS feed URL** — subscribe once in Google/Apple Calendar and confirmed games appear automatically
- Per-game .ics download

**Notifications:**
- Channels at launch: in-app inbox + email (reuses the weekly-email infrastructure)
- Event types: game proposed / confirmed / reminder (T-24h and T-1h) / rescheduled / overdue nudge / result posted / trade & claim events / commissioner announcements
- Per-user preferences: per-event-type toggles + optional daily digest

**Discord integration:**
- Commissioner pastes a Discord channel **webhook URL** into league settings — no bot, no OAuth, no Discord app approval. Webhooks accept plain HTTP POSTs, so this is near-zero dependency and cheap to build
- A league can register **multiple webhooks (up to 5)**, each with a label and an event-type filter — matching how league servers actually run: #scores gets results, #trades gets transactions, #announcements gets commissioner broadcasts
- **Send test message** button on save, so setup is verifiable in ten seconds
- Posts render as **rich embeds** (teams, score, players each way, timestamp) with a deep link back to the relevant Front Office page
- Architecturally, Discord is a **third sink on the existing notification dispatcher** alongside in-app inbox and email — same events, one more channel, no parallel system
- Delivery runs through an outbound queue with retry + backoff (Discord rate limits return 429 with a retry_after); repeated hard failures (deleted or revoked webhook → 404/401) auto-disable the webhook and notify the commissioner
- Webhook URLs are secrets: stored encrypted, shown masked after save, commissioner-only, changes logged (who/when)
- Optional toggle: overdue-game nudges can also post publicly to Discord (off by default — enable at your league's own risk)
- Backlog candidates: web push, full Discord bot (slash commands, role sync)

## 10. Results, Standings & History

- **Result reporting:** winner reports the score (optional box score attached); opponent confirms or disputes within a league-set window; auto-confirm on timeout (commissioner setting); disputes go to the commissioner queue with override authority — same shape as EA's force/override tools
- Derived standings from facts (existing discipline): team stats, league leaders, head-to-head records
- **Playoffs:** bracket generated from the Settings Sheet playoff format; series tracking and postseason stat views
- **[NEW] Records & History:** EA wipes annual stats, records, and awards at every season reset — Front Office doesn't. All-time standings, career stats per gamertag, championship banners, a league records book, and full season archives. This is the platform's moat.

## 11. Season Lifecycle

Mirrors EA's flow, with history intact on the site side:

Pre-League (provisioning, Settings Sheet, roster source lock) → Regular Season → Playoffs → Offseason (settings refinements, division realignment, keeper declarations, reset) → next season

- Offseason league-size reduction supported, matching EA's rule (removed non-human teams' players → free-agent pool, noted on the league log)

## 12. Communications *(existing + new touchpoints)*

- Weekly commissioner email from editable template — **[NEW]** auto-injected sections: unscheduled/overdue games, pending upload approvals, upcoming week's schedule
- Commissioner broadcast announcements → member inboxes + email

## 13. Data Discipline *(existing, applies everywhere)*

- Append-only facts; derived standings/cap views; provenance (actor, timestamp, source) on every fact
- Uploads, schedule changes, results, and transactions all conform — no exceptions for new modules

## Build Integration Notes

- **Dependencies:** §2 identity before league-type gating; §9 notification core before the scheduling flows that fire it; §6 uploads after the roster module exists
- **Suggested checkpoint grouping for BUILD_ORDER:** identity fields → Settings Sheet → uploads + approval queue → notification core → Discord sink → scheduling (availability → proposals → ICS → nudges) → results/disputes → history views
- **Efficiency:** scheduling is the largest new surface — split it into 3–4 checkpoints, one per fresh chat, plan-before-build per the EFFICIENCY guide
